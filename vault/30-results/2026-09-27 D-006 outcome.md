## Registered decisions, applied as written

- **B (keep DSE cache):** pooled CI upper 0.994 < 1 and 0 wrong, but 2 crashes → the rule's conditions are not all
  met → **registered verdict: drop DSE.** The addendum's general gate also fails (0.981 > 0.97).

  does not cause them. Under the registered text they still count. The speed evidence for DSE is positive on the pooled
  seeds (−1.9 %, CI clear of 1) — it is just not accepted under the rule written before the data.

## What this corrects
Seed 2 alone suggested the seed-1 3 % was mostly seed luck; seed 3 (0.963) says otherwise. Across seeds 1, 2 and 3 v4 vs
dev is 0.969, 0.990 and 0.963: a ~2.5 % effect, mostly from the DSE cache (search-changing), about 0.7 % from the
deterministic base. It does not pass this lab's 3 % gate, and the bohle memory cost is real at an 8 GiB cap.
